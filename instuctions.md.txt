
Step 1: Whisk the Batter

Combine the self-rising flour, egg, and milk in a large bowl. Whisk the ingredients together until the mixture is smooth and no large flour lumps remain.

Step 2: Heat the Pan

Warm a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium-low heat. Lightly grease the surface with a small pat of butter or cooking spray.

Step 3: Cook and Flip

Pour about 1/3 cup of batter onto the hot pan for each pancake. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until bubbles form and pop on the surface, then flip with a spatula and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown.
